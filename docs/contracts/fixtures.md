## 3. Findings that change the contract or the golden counts

These were not in `excel-analysis.md` and are reflected in the domain model:

1. **Two duplicate periods.** `HRS-CL-014-3059-30387` and `HRS-CL-005-3174-30354` each have two Complied rows due 2022-04-30, one under department "HR" and one under "Human Resources". Once the department alias is accepted they collide on `(assignmentId, periodStart)`. Contract: `DUPLICATE_PERIOD` issue (keep first row, the other row's date and comment become an IMPORT comment).
2. **One Complied row with no Activity Date**: `ENV-117-0031`, due 2022-09-30. Contract: `COMPLIED_WITHOUT_DATE` issue, proposal `completedOn = dueDate`.
3. **All 12 Not Applicable rows have an Activity Date.** Contract: for NOT_APPLICABLE, `completedOn` holds the date the NA was recorded; scores ignore it.
4. **Non-standard IDs**: six IDs do not follow the 5-part pattern: `ENV-117-0031`, `INT-FA-1-1`, `INT-H-047-0016`, `INT-H-048-0002`, `INT-H-050-0002`, `INT-H-056-0001`. Contract: `UNPARSED_EXTERNAL_REF` INFO issue.
5. **Department and owner differ within one ID** (2 IDs with two departments, 1 with two owners, 2 with two approvers). Contract: `CONFLICTING_OBLIGATION_VALUE`; per-row owner/approver stay on instances.
6. **Monthly due dates drift** (e.g. 14 May / 15 Jun, 29 Oct / 30 Nov). Expect `inferRule` to return a rule with `confidence < 1` and mismatches for these; the instances keep the Excel dates.

## 6. Firm B (synthetic)
Fully invented names and data. Firm "Second Ledger Consulting" with two clients:
- "Northwind Foods Group" (FY April), company "Northwind Foods Pvt Ltd", units "Pune Plant" (Maharashtra) and "Bengaluru Office" (Karnataka).
- "Harbour Retail Group" (`fyStartMonth = 1`, to prove the FY setting is honoured), company "Harbour Retail Pvt Ltd", unit "Chennai Store" (Tamil Nadu).

Contents:
- 15 obligations covering every `RecurrenceRule` kind, including `EVENT_DRIVEN` and `FIXED_DATES`, Central and State (MH, KA, TN) Acts.
- Instances for FY 2023-24 and FY 2024-25 (spans 29 Feb 2024 and both FY boundaries) in every `WorkflowState`, including SUBMITTED (both kinds), SENT_BACK, COMPLIED_LATE, reopened history, and one `dueDateOverridden`.
- Evidence in every `ScanStatus`; one obligation with `evidenceRequired`.
- People for every role: FA, two consultants (one assigned to Harbour only), CA, AP and OW per unit, an OW limited to one unit, a VIEWER with `expiresAt` in the past and one in the future, one UNCLAIMED person.

Firm B has the ISO add-on (`isoManagement`) with its catalogue copy recorded; Northwind's ISO data is in §8.

As-of date: `FIRM_B_AS_OF` (2024-11-15, exported from `@canopy/contracts/fixtures` and `@canopy/contracts/mock`). Nothing in the data happens after it. A mock serving firm B should pin `today` to it. With an earlier date (for example the fixture's creation date, 2023-03-15) nothing is due yet, so the score reads "No items due" while items approved later already count as complied, and no period before FY 2023-24 has any items. At this date it is FY 2024-25 Q3 for Northwind and FY 2024-25 Q4 for Harbour.

## 7. Personal data
The sample contains real employee names. The xlsx stays as the golden import file (root `CLAUDE.md`), but `firm-a.sample.json` replaces each person with a deterministic pseudonym ("Owner 01", "Approver 03", …) keyed by normalised name, so names do not spread into every test, snapshot and mock. Owner Comments are kept (they hold no names in this sample; re-checked when the JSON is generated).

The register's client and company are real organisations, so the JSON names them "Kestrel Metals Group" and "Kestrel Aluminium" instead. Their sites are replaced too: the unit's town becomes "Kestrelganj" and the other site named in owner comments becomes "Kestrelpur". The same substitution applies to text copied from the sheet (one Act names the company and its site). The generator fails if a real organisation or site name reaches any firm A file. Firm B's names are made up.

`isoCatalogueRows(firmId, at)` turns it into one account's rows as the copy writes them: the four catalogue tables,
the 4 STANDARD acts (CENTRAL, `kind: 'STANDARD'`), the category and one obligation per activity (`externalRef`
`ISO-<key>`, domain `OTHER`, `MEDIUM`, `TIME_BASED`, `CONFIRMED`, `evidenceRequired: true`, its `clauseRefs`). Ids
come from `isoCatalogueId(firmId, key)`: a UUIDv5 of `<firmId>:<key>` in `ISO_CATALOGUE_NAMESPACE`, so each account's
copy has its own ids and they are the same on every load. Keys: `standard:<code>:<edition>`,
`clause:<code>:<edition>:<number>`, `review-input:...`, `review-output:...`, `act:<key>`, `category`,
`obligation:<key>`. `isoClauseId(firmId, standard, number)` is the id of one clause, for example
`isoClauseId(firmId, 'ISO_9001', '7.1.5')`.
